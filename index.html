Stay connected to active work from anywhere

Codex in the ChatGPT mobile app is a fully-featured mobile experience for getting work done with Codex. When you connect to any of your machines where Codex is running, the app loads the live state from that environment so you can work fluidly across active threads, approvals, plugins, and project context.

Full thread management

Work across all of your threads from your phone. Review outputs, approve commands, change models, or start something new — all from a single interface.

Real-time updates

Files, credentials, permissions, and local setup stay on your machine while updates flow back to your phone in real time, including screenshots and terminal output.

Secure relay layer

Keeps trusted machines reachable across devices without exposing them to the public internet. Session state and context sync anywhere you are signed in.

Remote SSH support

Connect directly into managed remote environments with approved dependencies, credentials, and compute. Now generally available for all plans.

Step in when it matters

As Codex handles work over longer stretches, timely guidance becomes a bigger part of keeping that work useful. From your phone, you can start work when it is top of mind, unblock it when your judgment is needed, and stay close to the result as it takes shape.

01

Investigate a bug while waiting for coffee

Codex runs from your development environment — inspecting files, reproducing the issue, running tests, and beginning toward a fix. If it needs clarification, you can approve from your phone and follow along with screenshots and diffs before you are back at your computer.

02

Reach a decision point during your commute

Codex finds two viable approaches and needs your direction. From your phone, you review the tradeoffs, choose a path, and by the time you arrive, the task has kept moving in the direction you wanted.

03

Prepare for a fast-moving customer conversation

From your phone, ask Codex to synthesize the latest updates across Slack, email, documents, and browser-based tools — flagging key open questions and preparing a concise briefing before the call.

04

Turn a new idea into forward motion

Whether at lunch, out for a walk, or listening to something that sparks a thought, send it to Codex from your phone. The task can begin taking shape before you return to your desk.

Run Codex in enterprise environments

Many teams already develop inside managed remote environments that provide approved dependencies, credentials, security policies, and compute resources.

Programmatic access tokens

Scoped credentials issued directly from ChatGPT workspace settings for CI pipelines, release workflows, and internal automations.

Hooks

Scan prompts for secrets, run validators, log conversations, create memories, or customize Codex behavior for specific repositories and directories.

HIPAA-compliant support

Codex in local environments for ChatGPT Enterprise workspaces, enabling healthcare organizations to support patient care and operational workflows.

Remote SSH

The desktop app detects hosts from your SSH configuration and lets you create projects and run threads inside remote machines just like locally.
